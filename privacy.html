1. Introduction

Welcome to Vela. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

When we say “Vela”, “we”, “us” or “our”, we are referring to Vela Group S.L. and its affiliates. When we say “Services”, we mean our website, mobile applications, and related platform tools that link to this Policy.

For the processing activities described in this Policy, Vela Group S.L. acts as the data controller, unless stated otherwise for a specific service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

Account & profile details: such as your name, email address, phone number, and account credentials.
Service data: property information, booking or concierge details, preferences, and guest/invite details you submit through the platform.
Communications & uploads: support requests, feedback, and files/photos you upload (for example, property or dispute images).

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

Usage & device data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and interaction data needed to operate, secure, and improve our Services.
Cookies & consent preferences: including your cookie preference selections. See the Cookies section for more details.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third parties such as authentication providers (for example Google or Apple), payment processors (for payment status and transaction metadata), and service partners involved in delivering requested services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information primarily to provide, operate, and maintain the Vela Services. This includes personalising your experience, developing new features, communicating with you, ensuring the security of our platform, and complying with legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK only)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, we process your data based on the following legal grounds:

Contract

To fulfil our service agreement with you.

Consent

Where you have clearly given us permission.

Legitimate Interests

For business needs, balanced against your rights.

Legal Obligation

To comply with the law.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share personal data only where necessary with: (i) infrastructure and platform providers (including hosting, database, authentication, and storage services), (ii) payment processors, (iii) communication providers (such as transactional email delivery), (iv) professional advisors, and (v) authorities when required by law or to protect legal rights.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to run and secure our website and improve user experience. We use necessary cookies for core functionality and may use optional statistics/analytics and marketing cookies where consent is required by law. You can manage non-essential cookie choices through our consent interface when shown and through your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide Services, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with legal, accounting, and tax obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirement, after which data is deleted, anonymised, or securely archived where deletion is not immediately possible.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data; to request restriction of processing; to object to certain processing; and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Spain, the AEPD). To exercise your rights, please contact us.

9. Security

We implement technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we maintain layered controls designed to reduce risk.

How we protect your data in practice

  • Authenticated access: sensitive account and payment actions require a signed-in user session.
  • Server-side authorisation checks: backend functions validate user identity and ownership before data is returned or changed.
  • Payment security: payment processing is handled by Stripe, and we do not store full card numbers or CVC.
  • Webhook verification: Stripe webhook signatures are verified before payment and subscription events are accepted.
  • Revocable guest access: guest access links rely on random tokens and can be disabled by hosts.
  • Secrets management: API secrets are stored server-side and are not intended to be exposed in public website code.
  • Access limitation: we limit access to personal data to team members and providers who need it to deliver the service.

10. International Transfers

As a global service, some personal data may be processed outside your country. Where data is transferred internationally, we apply appropriate safeguards under applicable law, including European Commission adequacy decisions and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), together with supplementary measures where appropriate.

11. Children

Our Services are not directed to children. In Spain, we do not knowingly process personal data of children under 14 without appropriate parental or guardian authorisation. Where local law sets a higher age threshold, that higher threshold applies. If you believe a child has provided personal data without valid authorisation, please contact us so we can review and delete the data where required.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy occasionally. We’ll post changes here and update the “last updated” date.

13. Contact

If you have privacy questions or want to exercise your data protection rights, contact us at privacy@myvela.es.

If you are in Spain and believe your rights have not been addressed, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD): www.aepd.es.
